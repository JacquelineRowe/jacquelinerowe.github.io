How can we make it easier to identify human rights risks associated with technology projects, by both people without technical expertise and those without human rights expertise? I was involved in the early design of [Tech Harms Check](https://techharmscheck.org/), a tool released today which supports project managers
donors and practitioners to begin human rights due diligence processes for their projects and proposals involving technology. Congrats to the team and great to see this important tool out there making human rights due diligence more accessible for all!
